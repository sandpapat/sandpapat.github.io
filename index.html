P2 Research

Dissertation direction

Why does AI-generated Thai sound polite but cold?

English builds empathy mostly from emotion vocabulary. Thai carries much of it in grammar: sentence-final particles like นะ and เนอะ, set expressions like ไม่เป็นไร and สู้ ๆ, and leaving things unsaid. My hypothesis is that LLM Thai gets the grammar and politeness right but misses these interactional signals. I’m testing this in everyday comforting conversations.

  • Thai treebanks
  • Dependency parsing
  • Register variation
  • Affect & empathy in LLMs

P4 Garage

Corpus · 2026

ThaiTrees

A parsed corpus for comparing Thai syntax across news, Wikipedia, speech and social media. The pipeline runs CRFcut, AttaCut, AttaParse and a PhayaThaiBERT POS tagger I fine-tuned for the paper. The domains split into a formal pair (news and Wikipedia) and a conversational pair (speech and social media), by word frequency and by syntax.

90.64% POS accuracy 4.5M sentences 5,944 edge patterns
Paper GitHub

MSc dissertation · 2025

Thaislate

A translation tool that explains English tenses to Thai learners in Thai. Typhoon-Translate-4B translates, an XLM-RoBERTa classifier identifies the tense, and Typhoon-2.1-Instruct writes the explanation. It ran as a Flask app on GCP and was tested in an ethics-approved user study.

94.7% tense accuracy 38 participants 4.33/5 rating
